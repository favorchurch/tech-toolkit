## Known gaps (not represented as complete)

1. Full Git-history and asset audit across legacy documentation is outstanding.
2. Site-specific surveys and first assisted-launch rehearsals have not been recorded here.
3. Internet roles and stream timings in the Manila master SOP must not be generalized without local validation.
4. Website template lead times, request approvers, and integrations may change and require confirmation from its functional lead.
5. Engineering documentation is spread over many repositories; a full repo-by-repo coverage audit requires a separate pass.
6. Availability, accessibility, support response objectives, and budgets must be set by accountable owners rather than invented in this public document.
7. Authorized owners must establish the private campus addendum and evidence register before certification.
