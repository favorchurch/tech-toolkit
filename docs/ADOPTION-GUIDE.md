# Adoption guide for Functional Leads

**Status:** Proposed procedure to turn the first published toolkit into a validated operational standard. Do not represent the first public draft as completed field verification.

## First session: owner walkthrough

Internet Lead, Website Lead and Engineering Lead each review their section against **today's** team processes. For each important SOP step, classify:

- **Verified:** witnessed or tested in a representative environment; evidence exists.
- **Provisional:** supported by current documentation but not freshly demonstrated.
- **Proposed:** useful standard newly authored for this toolkit.
- **Deprecated:** no longer an approved procedure.

Record [document review](../templates/DOCUMENT-REVIEW.md), fix mismatches through reviewed PRs, and update training exercises.

## Second session: campus translation

Choose one representative campus and complete:
1. Site survey and service scope;
2. Named owner roles and authorized access;
3. Internet preflight/monitoring/packdown practice;
4. Website low-risk approved content exercise;
5. App issue triage and secure escalation simulation;
6. Restricted addendum reference without publishing live details.

Use [readiness matrix](../templates/CAMPUS-READINESS.md) to record results.

## Third session: Assisted Launch and certification

Operate actual services with Central Tech as an observer/supporter. Observe whether the local team can perform the task independently. Rehearse an appropriate non-destructive failure, verify end-to-end recovery, and record the next training or equipment gap.

Issue the first scoped certificate only after Central Tech validates actual evidence through [certification](../launch/CERTIFICATION.md).

## Review when done

- Which steps required undocumented help?
- Which page was hard to find on an actual service day?
- What sensitive information should be removed or moved to restricted storage?
- Which equipment or access assumptions failed in a different campus?
- Which checklist can be shorter without losing a safety gate?
- Who owns the first revisit date?

Keep the public guides portable while updating private addenda for actual site differences.
